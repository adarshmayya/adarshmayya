## AI research interests

I want to understand how AI systems can retrieve useful evidence, act through tools and communicate their limitations reliably. My current interests grow out of building and evaluating my own RAG and agent projects:

- **Retrieval and grounded generation:** how chunking, embeddings, reranking and context selection affect answer quality and source attribution.
- **Agent reliability:** evaluating tool use, state management and multi-step workflows, including how errors propagate and when explicit rules are more appropriate.
- **Evaluation and reproducibility:** comparing approaches against clear baselines, testing failure cases, and making experiments traceable through datasets, metrics and documentation.
- **AI for knowledge and scientific workflows:** exploring assistants that help people find, compare and use information while keeping supporting evidence accessible.

My goal is to combine practical software engineering with applied AI research and contribute to useful, testable systems in collaboration with researchers and domain experts.

## Featured projects

### [Enterprise Agentic RAG](https://github.com/adarshmayya/Enterprise-RAG)

A document-question-answering application with source-grounded responses and an evaluation workflow.

- Ingests documents, generates embeddings, and combines Qdrant vector search with semantic reranking.
- Uses LangGraph to coordinate planning, retrieval and response generation, with guardrails and model fallbacks.
- Includes a FastAPI backend, Streamlit interface, RAGAS evaluation, tracing and container configuration.

**Built with:** Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, Qdrant, RAGAS, Streamlit, Docker.

### [Financial Decision Agent](https://github.com/adarshmayya/financial-decision-agent)

A learning project exploring multi-agent reasoning through a structured financial research workflow.

- Coordinates web research, opposing analyses and a debate step using Google ADK and Gemini.
- Combines model-generated analysis with deterministic risk scoring and structured output.
- Persists sessions in SQLite and documents practical lessons from implementing agent tools and state handling.

**Built with:** Python, Google ADK, Gemini, SQLite.
